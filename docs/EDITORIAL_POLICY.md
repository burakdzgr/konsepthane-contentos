# Konsepthane ContentOS - Editorial Policy

## Policy objective

ContentOS must help produce useful, original, evidence-backed Turkish content
without hiding uncertainty, misrepresenting authorship or experience, or using
media without rights. Policy failures are recorded and routed for correction;
they are not silently ignored.

## Originality

- Research must synthesize multiple relevant sources and add a Konsepthane-specific
  angle, structure, explanation, comparison, or practical value.
- Translating, lightly rewriting, or following the heading structure of one
  competitor article is prohibited.
- Similarity and duplicate checks must cover existing Konsepthane content as well
  as discovered external material.
- Common facts may be reported with evidence, but another publisher's expression,
  selection, or arrangement must not be copied.

## Sourcing and evidence

- Prefer primary, official, expert, and current sources appropriate to the claim.
- Material claims require a traceable source, capture time, and the exact evidence
  excerpt or structured fact used by the editorial team.
- Evidence packs must distinguish observation, source assertion, inference, and
  editorial judgment.
- Source disagreement, uncertainty, stale data, and geographic limits must be visible.
- A source URL alone is not evidence that a claim was verified.
- AI output is never a source.

## Copyright and text use

- Store only the source material necessary for research, verification, and audit,
  subject to applicable terms and retention rules.
- Do not republish substantial passages, protected tables, illustrations, or
  distinctive article structures without permission.
- Quotations must be necessary, limited, accurate, attributed, and permitted.
- Crawl permission does not grant republication rights.

## Images and other media

Every asset requires provenance and one of these policy states:

- `FIRST_PARTY`
- `LICENSED_USABLE`
- `PUBLIC_DOMAIN`
- `AI_GENERATED`
- `REFERENCE_ONLY`
- `UNKNOWN_LICENSE`

The record must include source/creator, license or rights basis, proof location,
allowed uses, required attribution, transformations, reviewer, and expiry where
applicable. `UNKNOWN_LICENSE` and `REFERENCE_ONLY` assets cannot be included in
an automatic publication package.

Pinterest, Instagram, competitor-site, and search-result images are reference-only
unless usage rights are separately verified. Embedding, downloading, cropping,
redrawing, or prompting from an image does not by itself create usage rights.

## AI-generated media

- AI-generated assets must be labeled `AI_GENERATED` internally with provider,
  model, generation time, prompt/provenance record, and human reviewer.
- Generated media must be checked for misleading realism, trademarks, recognizable
  persons, unsafe content, visual plagiarism risk, and factual misrepresentation.
- AI media must not be presented as documentary evidence, a real event photograph,
  a customer submission, or a real person's experience.
- Publication and disclosure rules must follow applicable law, platform policy,
  and Konsepthane editorial standards in force at review time.

## Fake UGC prohibition

ContentOS must not invent testimonials, comments, ratings, before/after stories,
first-person experiences, interviews, quotations, or community posts. Synthetic
content must never be framed as user-generated content. Real UGC requires
traceable consent, provenance, moderation, and permitted reuse.

## Factual claims

- Numbers, dates, legal/medical/financial statements, product specifications,
  safety claims, and claims about people or organizations require heightened review.
- Time-sensitive claims must include an as-of date and be refreshed or removed
  when no longer supportable.
- Calculations and transformations must preserve inputs, method, units, and rounding.
- Unsupported claims are removed or returned to research; plausible wording is
  not a substitute for evidence.
- Content must clearly separate fact, estimate, recommendation, and opinion.

## Editorial quality

Publishable work must:

- satisfy the approved brief and the user's search intent;
- provide specific, usable value rather than generic filler;
- use clear, natural Turkish appropriate to the audience;
- avoid keyword stuffing, deceptive titles, unsupported superlatives, and false urgency;
- disclose material limitations, conflicts, commercial relationships, and uncertainty;
- use accurate links, headings, metadata, and accessible media text;
- pass duplicate, evidence, QA, media, and governance gates.

SEO supports discovery but cannot override accuracy, originality, rights, or reader value.

## Human review and approval

- Auto-publishing is disabled at launch.
- An authorized human must review the exact content, evidence, QA report, and media
  versions included in the publication package.
- Approval, requested changes, and rejection require actor, timestamp, scope, and reason.
- Substantive post-approval changes invalidate approval and require renewed QA/review.
- Human reviewers cannot waive unknown-license media, fabricated evidence, fake UGC,
  or the prohibition on direct production database publishing.
- Any later auto-publishing policy requires observed quality data, explicit risk
  thresholds, rollback/kill controls, sampled human review, and a new accepted ADR.
